ABOUT MY WORK

I like engineering problems that end with real hardware.

My work spans mechanical design, composite structures, manufacturing, structural testing, and experimental engineering.

I am most interested in projects where I can stay close to the hardware: understanding the problem, developing a solution, building it, testing it, figuring out what went wrong, and improving the next version.
